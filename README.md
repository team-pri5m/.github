## 👋 About Us

**Team Pri5m** is a cybersecurity and CTF team from Nepal. We learn by breaking things, building things, documenting what we discover, and competing against problems designed to test practical security skills.

Our work spans offensive security, reverse engineering, digital forensics, cryptography, web exploitation, binary exploitation, OSINT, mobile security, hardware/IoT security, and challenge development.

We also work on **HimalCTF**, including the systems, workflows, documentation, and challenge-development process needed to run a structured CTF event.

## 🧩 What We Do

* **Capture The Flag** — compete in jeopardy-style, attack-defense, and specialized security challenges.
* **Security Research** — investigate vulnerabilities, protocols, applications, binaries, mobile apps, and unusual systems.
* **Challenge Development** — design reproducible CTF challenges with intentional attack paths, validation, and documentation.
* **Writeups & Knowledge Sharing** — document techniques, tooling, lessons learned, and challenge solutions when disclosure is appropriate.
* **Security Tooling** — build scripts, solvers, automation, infrastructure, and utilities that support research and competitions.
* **Community Projects** — collaborate on initiatives that help strengthen Nepal's cybersecurity and CTF ecosystem.

## 🏔️ HimalCTF

Team Pri5m is developing **HimalCTF** as a structured CTF initiative with an emphasis on challenge quality, reproducibility, author workflows, technical review, and fair competition.

Our event-development workflow covers:

* challenge proposals and approval;
* category-specific development standards;
* repository and issue-based collaboration;
* testing and validation;
* deployment readiness;
* author documentation;
* challenge release and maintenance;
* post-event archival and writeups.

> Challenge material may remain private before or during an event to protect competition integrity.

## 🧭 How We Work

We value:

* **Technical depth** over superficial solutions.
* **Reproducibility** — a result should be explainable and verifiable.
* **Documentation** — discoveries are more useful when others can learn from them.
* **Collaboration** — different security disciplines are stronger when combined.
* **Responsible research** — work should remain within authorized systems, competitions, labs, and clearly permitted scopes.
* **Continuous learning** — every challenge is an opportunity to improve methodology and tooling.

## ⚠️ Responsible Use

Repositories under this organization may contain offensive-security concepts, proof-of-concept code, exploit-development material, or CTF techniques.

They are intended for **authorized security research, education, competitions, and lab environments**. Always obtain permission before testing systems you do not own or explicitly control.
